Gardensin the Sky

+Type : Residential (Architecture)
+Location: Calicut, Kerala, India

Gardens in the Sky is a 3,500-square-foot residential duplex in Calicut, Kerala, designed as a fluid, vertically connected space centered around openness and a constant relationship with nature. The lower level forms the social core with interconnected living, dining, and kitchen areas, while the upper level houses private bedrooms linked by a bridge. Key architectural highlights include a striking, sculptural red staircase that functions as the spatial spine of the home, a quiet prayer nook with custom metalwork, and lush peripheral gardens lining the balconies, creating the lived experience of a green sanctuary suspended above the city.

WOASecond Home

+Type : Office space
+Location: Kochi, Kerala, India

Second Home is a 1,450-square-foot tropical brutalist workspace in Ernakulam, Kerala, designed as a fluid, open-plan studio centred around community, collaboration, and a constant relationship with nature. The space embraces a restrained material palette of exposed concrete, high-density fibreboard, and matte steel, balanced by over 100 potted tropical plants that provide natural ventilation, air purification, and acoustic comfort. Key architectural highlights include custom 240 cm ferrocement workstations, a dynamic pegboard atelier wall, and a central discussion zone featuring live furniture prototyping, creating the lived experience of a biophilic sanctuary that feels more like a home than a traditional office.
